# Privacy Policy — BabeBasket Poster

Last updated: 23 November 2026

BabeBasket Poster is an internal tool of the shop **Babe Basket**. It publishes the shop's own posts (photos and
captions about drugstore products) to the shop's own accounts on Threads, Instagram and the Facebook Page "Babe Basket".
It is used only by the shop's owners. It is not offered to other people or businesses.

## What data the app uses
- **Access tokens** for the shop's own Threads, Instagram and Facebook Page accounts, so that it can publish posts there.
  They are stored only on the shop's own computer and are never shared.
- **Basic information about the shop's own accounts** (account ID, username, Page ID and name), needed to publish.
- **The posts the shop publishes** (images and captions) and their links, kept so a post is never published twice.

## What the app does not do
- It does not collect, read, store or sell personal data of customers, followers or any other Facebook, Instagram or
  Threads users.
- It does not read messages, comments or profiles of other people.
- It does not use data for advertising and does not share data with third parties.

## Retention and deletion
Tokens and post records stay on the shop's computer for as long as the shop uses the tool, and are deleted when the
tool is removed or access is revoked. See [Data deletion instructions](data-deletion.md).

## Contact
Questions about this policy: send a message to the Facebook Page **Babe Basket** or to Instagram **@babe._basket**.
